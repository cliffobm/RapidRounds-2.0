# CJIS assessment evidence policy (draft)

- Keep Rapid Round game points and response speed separate from competency estimates.
- Never claim validated clinical competency based on uncalibrated items.
- Record first attempts, hints, definitions, elapsed time, case family, item version, and response outcome.
- Repeated answers to the same item are practice, not independent evidence.
- Responses within one case family are dependent; do not naively count them as independent evidence.
- Assisted responses require explicit classification and must not silently receive the same evidence weight as unassisted responses.
- Timeouts are nonresponses unless the assessment specification explicitly justifies another treatment.
- Safety-critical misses trigger education and reassessment; they are not proof of incompetence.
- All clinical items require subject-matter review and references before scored production release.
